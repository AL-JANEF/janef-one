## Promotion rule

A rule enters the canonical core only if it is:
- portable across runtimes;
- compatible with actual higher-priority policy;
- operationally useful;
- non-duplicative;
- testable or clearly verifiable;
- not dependent on a stale provider identity or unavailable tool.
